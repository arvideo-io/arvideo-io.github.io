PROOF BEFORE THE WIPE

Three stamps, in order.

Every file on the media gets a SHA-256 checksum, and each one is checked against its copy in your storage system — content proven, not just names, so nothing is ever suggested for deletion on a filename match alone. The stamps land as the work finishes. The last one means Sender itself can do the wipe, behind a confirmation, and it will only ever delete files whose checksums verified. Anything unverified stays on the media, always.
