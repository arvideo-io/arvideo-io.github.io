NORMAL

Title card, clean picture, no frills.

OVERLAY ± BOXES

Facts on the picture — with or without detection boxes drawn live.

STAR

Night-sky events only. Stars are plate-solved against the catalog — Hipparcos · IAU · Skyfield — local detection.
